Only visible at low tide, the Sunken Temple's carvings predate every
kingdom that ever claimed the isles. [The Drowned Concord](/campaigns/sunken-isles/articles/drowned-concord)
has been excavating its lower galleries for months, hauling relics out
through routes that conveniently avoid
[Saltmere](/campaigns/sunken-isles/articles/saltmere)'s harbor watch.

Anyone diving here at night reports the same thing: the carvings are warm
to the touch, and they're arranged differently every time.
