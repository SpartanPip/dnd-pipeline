Ravenspire Keep sits on the tallest of the Sunken Isles, its lower levels
long since claimed by the tide. What remains above water is held by a
garrison that stopped taking orders from the mainland generations ago.

Lately the keep has been buying up salvage and old relics through
middlemen in [Saltmere](/campaigns/sunken-isles/articles/saltmere) —
nobody's sure why, but some suspect it has to do with whatever
[The Drowned Concord](/campaigns/sunken-isles/articles/drowned-concord)
is digging for at [The Sunken Temple](/campaigns/sunken-isles/articles/sunken-temple).
