The Drowned Concord believes the isles sank on purpose — offered up to
something that sleeps beneath
[The Sunken Temple](/campaigns/sunken-isles/articles/sunken-temple). Its
members surface in Saltmere only to buy supplies or recruit the desperate.

Their goals overlap suspiciously with
[Ravenspire Keep](/campaigns/sunken-isles/articles/ravenspire-keep)'s
salvage requisitions, though whether the keep's garrison and the Concord
are allies, pawns, or rivals is still an open question at the table.
