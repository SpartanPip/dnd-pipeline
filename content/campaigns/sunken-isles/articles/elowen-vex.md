Elowen Vex captains the *Grey Petrel* and holds more influence over
[Saltmere](/campaigns/sunken-isles/articles/saltmere) than anyone with an
actual title. She's pragmatic, well-armed, and deeply suspicious of
[Ravenspire Keep](/campaigns/sunken-isles/articles/ravenspire-keep)'s recent
salvage buying spree.

She'll trade favors, information, or ship passage — but she always
collects. Adventurers who cross her once rarely get a second chance to dock
in Saltmere.
