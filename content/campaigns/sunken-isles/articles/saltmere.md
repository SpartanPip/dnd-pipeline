Saltmere is a ramshackle port town built on stilts over a flooded harbor,
the only settlement in the isles that hasn't sworn allegiance to a pirate
fleet or a drowned god. Its docks are run by a loose council of captains,
chief among them [Captain Elowen Vex](/campaigns/sunken-isles/articles/elowen-vex).

Rumors travel fast here: dockhands whisper about lights under the water
near [The Sunken Temple](/campaigns/sunken-isles/articles/sunken-temple),
and about strange requisitions coming out of
[Ravenspire Keep](/campaigns/sunken-isles/articles/ravenspire-keep). Most
adventures in the isles start with a drink in Saltmere's one working tavern.
